# Code of conduct

## Our standard

This is a small documentation repository. The standard is simple: be straightforward and be accurate.

**Expected**

- Say what you observed and how you verified it
- Disagree with the content, not the person
- Accept that a correction to your contribution is about the documentation, not about you

**Not accepted**

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments
- Publishing anyone's private information
- Posting another company's ad account data, customer data, or credentials
- Deliberately misleading claims about what this or any competing tool can do

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, and any space where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **conduct@portermetrics.com**. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that break this standard, and may block repeat offenders.
