# Support

## Something is not working

1. **Check [errors](06-reference/errors.md)** — real Meta error subcodes and their fixes.
2. **Check [what it cannot do](06-reference/what-it-cannot-do.md)** — the honest list of limits, verified against the live connector.
3. **Check the setup guide for your client** in [`05-connect/`](05-connect/) — most connection problems are client-specific.

## Still stuck

**Open an issue** in this repository for anything about the documentation: a wrong field, a prompt that fails, a missing error.

**Contact Porter** for anything about the service itself — your account, your connected data sources, billing, or a platform outage:

- Help centre: [help.portermetrics.com](https://help.portermetrics.com)
- Email: support@portermetrics.com

## Asking a good question

Include the exact question you typed, the response you got, which AI client you were using, and your ad account's currency. Budget minimums and cost figures differ by currency, and that explains a surprising share of confusing answers.
